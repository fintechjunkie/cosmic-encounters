# Art direction

The game uses fully original imagery generated with OpenAI's built-in ImageGen tool. The visual thesis is a tactile late-1970s science-fiction paperback: deep teal space, burnt-orange nebulae, airbrushed forms, coarse screen-print stipple, and restrained analog grain. It evokes the period without copying the box, cards, aliens, or other protected artwork from any edition of *Cosmic Encounter*.

## Production assets

- `site/public/art/cosmic-board.webp` — panoramic board backdrop
- `site/public/art/alien-portraits.webp` — 2-by-2 atlas: Clone, Macron, Virus, Zombie
- `site/public/art/celestial-atlas.webp` — 3-by-2 atlas of six planets and moons
- `site/public/art/alien-power-cards.webp` — ornate 2-by-2 power-card atlas
- `site/public/art/encounter-symbols.webp` — 3-by-2 encounter-card symbol atlas
- `site/public/art/cosmic-vortex.webp` — animated board centerpiece

The contact sheets are rendered as CSS sprite atlases. This keeps the game page fast while giving every alien and planet a distinct illustration.

## Final prompts

### Cosmic board

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide atmospheric website table-board backdrop, final bitmap asset. Primary request: an original panoramic deep-space starfield with a broad burnt-orange nebula drifting through teal-black space, evoking a cosmic tabletop arena without depicting any game components. Scene/backdrop: layered teal-black interstellar darkness, sparse wisps of burnt-orange gas, tiny varied stars and faint dust. Style/medium: vintage late-1970s science-fiction paperback airbrush illustration with subtle screen-print grain, tactile ink texture, restrained retro color separation, fully original. Composition/framing: exact-feeling 3:1 ultra-wide panorama; nebula flows diagonally across the frame while the center remains calm enough to serve as a board backdrop; edge-to-edge art. Lighting/mood: mysterious, warm nebular glow against cool dark space, atmospheric and dramatic. Color palette: burnt orange, rust, ochre, deep teal, blue-black, small ivory star points. Materials/textures: fine screen-print stipple, gentle airbrush gradients, slight analog paper grain. Constraints: no text, no typography, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no cards, no tokens, no board-game pieces, no people, no creatures, no copyrighted characters, no recognizable franchise imagery; original artwork only.

### Alien portraits

Primary request: four clearly separated, fully original alien bust portraits representing four distinct archetypes: Clone, Macron, Virus, and Zombie. Scene/backdrop: one dark simple background per quadrant with generous separation between subjects. Subject: TOP LEFT Clone — an elegant duplicated humanoid alien with subtly mirrored facial anatomy, porcelain-cyan skin and twin cranial ridges; TOP RIGHT Macron — a massive broad-shouldered alien bust with a huge domed skull, basalt skin and gold-orange eyes; BOTTOM LEFT Virus — a spindly bio-mechanical alien with a translucent spore crown, acid-green membrane details and narrow face; BOTTOM RIGHT Zombie — an ancient reanimated extraterrestrial with desiccated violet-gray skin, hollow luminous eyes and ragged ceremonial collar. These descriptions are original and should not resemble existing game art. Style/medium: vintage late-1970s science-fiction paperback cover airbrush illustration with screen-print texture, visible analog ink grain, bold shadow shapes, limited retro palette. Composition/framing: square canvas divided visually into an exact clean 2-by-2 grid; four equal quadrants; one centered head-and-shoulders bust in each quadrant; strong readable silhouette; clearly separated subjects; consistent portrait scale; no overlap across cells. Lighting/mood: dramatic rim light, mysterious pulpy science-fiction atmosphere. Color palette: deep teal-black and charcoal backgrounds, cyan, burnt orange, acid green, violet-gray accents. Materials/textures: soft airbrush modeling, coarse screen-print stipple, slight paper grain. Constraints: exactly four portraits and exactly one bust per quadrant; no labels, no words, no letters, no numbers, no typography, no logos, no watermark, no card borders, no frames, no UI; no copyrighted characters; do not imitate or reproduce any existing game artwork; fully original designs.

Lighting/mood: hypnotic, dangerous, inviting, energetic tabletop spectacle. Color palette: deep teal-black, cyan, turquoise, burnt orange, rust, ochre, bone-white star sparks. Materials/textures: layered airbrush haze, screen-print dots, rough dry-ink edges, subtle paper tooth. Constraints: exactly one central vortex; no text, no labels, no letters, no numbers, no typography, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no card frames, no tokens, no copyrighted characters, no recognizable franchise elements; do not imitate or reproduce vintage Cosmic Encounter artwork; fully original design.
