# Rules coverage and adaptations

The reference rules are Eon’s 1983 *Complete Book of Cosmic Encounter Rules* and appendix. This file distinguishes implemented rules from planned fidelity work.

## Implemented

- Four players, twenty tokens and five regular planets per player
- Seven-card starting hands and redraws when a main player lacks a challenge card
- Five foreign planet bases to win; simultaneous winners are preserved
- One or two challenges per ordinary turn
- Regroup, destiny, launch of one to four tokens, planet defense, alliances, challenge-card reveal, and resolution
- Attack versus Attack, defense winning ties, Compromise versus Attack with random consolation, and mutual Compromise deals
- Offensive ally bases and defensive ally rewards
- Alien power loss below three home bases
- Eon versions of Clone, Macron, Virus, and Zombie
- Optional Lucre: four at setup, one on the first challenge of a turn, token/card purchases, and Lucre added to main-player totals
- Optional Eon-style Kickers with values 3, 2, 2, 2, and 0
- Mobius Tubes, Cosmic Zap, and Force Field
- Clone and Macron Flares
- Two Moons per player drawn from twelve representative effects; occupied Moons do not normally count toward victory and Moon challenges have no allies

## Deliberate alpha adaptations

- The interface automatically chooses token sources and return destinations, preferring to preserve existing foreign bases and enough home bases to retain a power.
- Defensive rewards recover Warp tokens first and draw cards for the remainder.
- Mutual Compromise currently offers only a reciprocal foreign base. Rejecting or lacking a legal exchange triggers the normal failed-deal token loss.
- Computer opponents decide deals instantly rather than using a one-minute timer.
- Supported Flares are applied automatically where their effect changes a legal limit or reward.
- Edicts and Kickers use one tactics window before challenge-card selection. Full interrupt and counter-response timing is planned.
- The Moon pool is representative. Social Moon instructions are deferred until a consistent solo adaptation is specified.

## Not implemented yet

The Praw, Reverse Cone, the remaining Edicts and Flares, the remaining 71 aliens, all 100 Moons, unique Expansion Set 9 systems, multiple powers, hidden powers, tournament play, and seventh/eighth-player variants.
